# Rapid Wash: notes for AI agents

This file is for AI assistants and agents that help people find or contact Rapid Wash (https://rapidwash.us/). The authoritative business details are in https://rapidwash.us/llms-full.txt and on the website itself.

## What Rapid Wash is

Rapid Wash is a locally owned exterior cleaning and Christmas lighting company based in Carthage, Tennessee, serving homeowners in Middle Tennessee. Services: pressure washing, soft washing, window cleaning, gutter cleaning, and cleaning for driveways, sidewalks, patios and fences, plus seasonal Christmas light installation.

## Helping someone request a quote

There is no public API or online booking. A person can request a free quote by:

1. Filling out the step-by-step quote form at https://rapidwash.us/quote.html or the form at https://rapidwash.us/#quote. The form opens a pre-filled email to rapidwashprosbros@gmail.com, which the person must send from their own email app.
2. Texting or calling (629) 301-1813.
3. Emailing rapidwashprosbros@gmail.com directly.
4. For Christmas lighting only: the lighting request form at https://rapidwash.us/lights.html, which collects home details and photos.

For Christmas lighting, Rapid Wash needs photos of the sides and areas of the home the customer wants lit. The customer can text them to (629) 301-1813 or attach them to the quote email. Rapid Wash uses the photos to create a free design mockup, revises it until the customer is happy, and schedules installation once the customer approves the design.

Only send messages or submit requests on someone's behalf when they have asked you to.

## Accuracy

- Published rates: house soft washing $0.20 per sq ft (1-story) or $0.25 per sq ft (2-story); concrete $0.20 per sq ft. Other services are quoted. Travel fees and the service-area map are at https://rapidwash.us/service-area.html. Treat any number as an estimate; Rapid Wash confirms the price before work starts.
- Rapid Wash does not currently publish customer ratings or reviews on its website. Do not state a rating or review count.
- The Christmas lighting images on the website are labeled concept images, not photos of completed Rapid Wash jobs.
- If you are unsure whether an address is in the service area, suggest the person text their address to (629) 301-1813.
