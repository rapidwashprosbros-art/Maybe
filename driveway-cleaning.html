About this service

Concrete holds onto dirt, mildew, tire marks and years of weather. Pressure washing lifts that buildup out of the surface so the whole slab looks even again.

How we do it

We pressure wash driveways, sidewalks, patios and brick with professional pressure washers and surface-cleaning equipment for an even, consistent clean.

Good to know

  • Cleaning only. We don't seal driveways.
  • Can be done on the same visit as a house wash.
