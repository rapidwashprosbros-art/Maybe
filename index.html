How We Clean

Soft wash the house. Pressure wash the concrete.

Different surfaces need different methods. High pressure on siding can damage it, and low pressure on a driveway barely touches it. We match the method to the surface.

Low pressure

Siding, brick, trim

A cleaning solution does the work, then a gentle rinse.

 Full pressure

Driveways, sidewalks, patios

Professional pressure washers and surface cleaners for an even finish.

 Right pressure

Decks, fences, gutters

Matched to the surface so it comes clean without damage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about Rapid Wash, our services, and how quotes work.

What services does Rapid Wash offer?

Rapid Wash offers year-round exterior cleaning: pressure washing, soft washing, window cleaning, gutter cleaning, and cleaning for driveways, sidewalks, patios and fences. Pressure washing is available for homes and businesses. Rapid Wash also installs Christmas lights during the holiday season.

Does Rapid Wash pressure wash driveways?

Yes. Rapid Wash pressure washes driveways, sidewalks and patios using professional pressure washers and surface-cleaning equipment.

Does Rapid Wash soft wash houses?

Yes. Soft washing is one of Rapid Wash's exterior cleaning services. We use the right pressure and cleaning solutions for different surfaces. Request a free quote and tell us what you'd like cleaned.

Does Rapid Wash clean windows and gutters?

Yes. Window cleaning and gutter cleaning are both part of Rapid Wash's exterior cleaning services.

What areas does Rapid Wash serve?

Rapid Wash is based in Carthage, Tennessee, and serves homeowners in Middle Tennessee. If you're not sure whether you're in our area, text your address to (629) 301-1813.

How much does Rapid Wash charge?

Quotes are free. House soft washing is $0.20 per square foot for 1-story homes and $0.25 per square foot for 2-story homes. Driveways, sidewalks, patios and other concrete are $0.20 per square foot. Windows, gutters, decks, fences and new construction cleanup are quoted after we see the property. We confirm your price before any work starts.

Does Rapid Wash charge a travel fee?

There is no travel fee within 30 miles of Carthage, Tennessee. From 30 to 45 miles away it is $25 per visit, and from 45 to 55 miles away it is $50 per visit. If you are farther than 55 miles, text your address to (629) 301-1813 and ask.

Does Rapid Wash serve Carthage, TN?

Yes. Rapid Wash is based in Carthage, Tennessee.

How do I get a quote?

Quotes are free. Fill out the quote form on this page or text (629) 301-1813. For Christmas lighting, also send photos of the sides and areas of your home you want lit. Text them to (629) 301-1813 or attach them to your quote email.

What happens after I request a quote?

The quote form opens a pre-filled email in your email app. Press Send so it reaches Rapid Wash, and we'll follow up about your project. For Christmas lighting, we use your photos to create your free mockup, revise it until you're happy, and schedule installation once you approve the design.

How can I contact Rapid Wash?

Call or text (629) 301-1813, or email rapidwashprosbros@gmail.com.

Does Rapid Wash install Christmas lights?

Yes. Rapid Wash installs Christmas lights for homes in Middle Tennessee. You send photos of the areas you want lit, we create a free design mockup, you can request changes, and once you approve the design we schedule your job and install the lights.

Is the Christmas lighting mockup free?

Yes. The design mockup is free, and you can request changes before anything is installed. Nothing goes up until you've approved the design.
